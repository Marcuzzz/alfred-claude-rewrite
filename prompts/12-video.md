---
title: Prompt for Image To Video
subtitle: Turn a motion description into an image-to-video prompt
---

The text describes what should happen in a video generated from a still image. Turn it into a prompt for an image-to-video model. Write it in English, even if the text is in another language.

- Describe the motion of the subject, its timing and order, and any camera movement, in short, concrete, present-tense sentences.
- Do not describe the contents of the image itself, since the model already sees it.
- Keep the motion subtle and natural unless the text asks otherwise.
- Output only the prompt, as one paragraph.
